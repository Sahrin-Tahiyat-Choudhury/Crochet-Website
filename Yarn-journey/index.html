At TheYarnJourney, every product is handmade with care and made to order. Due to the nature of handmade items, we follow a strict return and refund policy.
1 No Returns or Exchanges
All orders are final. We do not accept returns or exchanges once the order is confirmed and processed.
2 No Refund Policy
Payments are non-refundable once the order is placed. Please review all product details carefully before confirming your purchase.
3 Eligible Refunds (Limited Cases Only)
  • The product is damaged during transit
  • You receive an incorrect or incomplete order (missing items)
4 Exclusions
Refunds will not be provided for:
  • Change of mind after purchase
  • Minor variations in color, shape, or size (all items are handmade)
  • Differences caused by lighting or photography
  • Shipping charges or bouquet wrapping charges
5 Unboxing Video Requirement
To request a refund or claim:
  • Record a continuous, uncut unboxing video from start to finish
  • The parcel must be clearly visible from all angles before opening
  • All items must be shown and checked in the same video
⚠️ The unboxing video is mandatory proof. Claims without this will not be accepted.
6 Claim Process
  • Share the unboxing video within 24 hours of delivery
  • Send via WhatsApp or Instagram DM
  • After verification, your claim will be reviewed
7 Resolution (If Approved)
  • Replacement of the product, or
  • Store credit (depending on product availability)
At TheYarnJourney, every product is handmade with care. Please read our shipping policy before placing your order.
1 Order Processing Time
  • Orders are processed only after successful payment confirmation
  • Making process takes 3–14 days depending on the product
  • Processing time may vary during high order volume
  • Orders are processed in the sequence they are received
  • Need urgent delivery? Contact us on Instagram before placing the order
2 Shipping Charges
  • Shipping charges are calculated separately and added at checkout
  • The final shipping cost will be shown before order confirmation
3 Delivery Timeline
  • Once dispatched, delivery usually takes 5–13 days depending on your location
  • Delays caused by courier services are beyond our control
4 Dispatch & Tracking
  • Orders are dispatched after the product is completed
  • Once shipped, you will receive a Tracking ID and courier details
5 Address & Contact Details
  • Ensure your shipping address and contact number are complete and accurate
  • Changes allowed only within 24 hours of placing the order
  • No changes can be made after dispatch
6 Incorrect Address / Re-shipping
  • If parcel is returned due to incorrect address, re-shipping charges are paid by the customer
  • Orders re-dispatched only after additional shipping cost is cleared
7 Delivery Issues & Delays
  • Once dispatched, delivery is handled by the courier partner
  • We are not responsible for delays due to logistics, weather, or courier issues
  • Contact us and we will try to assist from our side
8 Missing or Undelivered Packages
  • If marked delivered but not received, contact us within 48 hours
  • Requests raised after this period may not be accepted
  • Final resolution depends on the courier partner's investigation

Our Story

The Heart Behind
The Yarn

TheYarnJourney is a small handmade brand run by a single creator who turned a love for crochet into something meaningful.

Every flower you see here is carefully made by hand, one piece at a time, with patience and attention to detail. What started as a personal passion has slowly grown into a space where creativity meets emotion — creating flowers that don't fade and gifts that last.

Behind the scenes, this journey is supported by close friends who helped bring this website to life, making it easier to share these handmade creations with you.

This is not a big business — it's a small, growing journey built with honesty, effort, and a genuine love for handmade art. Thank you for being a part of it. 🌸
